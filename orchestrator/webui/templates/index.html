MRAG Agent

MRAG (Master Regulator analysis of Accessible Genome) integrates ATAC-seq (chromatin accessibility) and RNA-seq (gene expression) to identify the transcription factors that drive condition-specific gene regulation.

  • Chat — the main entry point. Launch and monitor runs through natural conversation with the agent.
